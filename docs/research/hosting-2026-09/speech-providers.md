# Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech providers for a bilingual (en-US / es-MX) clinical voice companion

Prepared 2026-09-10. All prices are list/public self-serve prices unless noted; enterprise/committed-volume pricing is usually lower. All dollar figures are USD. "Accessed 2026-09-10" applies to every citation unless a different date is shown next to the link.

## 1. Scope and method

This report covers the STT and TTS providers named in the research brief, for a supervised clinical decision-support product that is synthetic-data-only today and may need a BAA later. It draws on official pricing/docs pages where they were fetchable, and on secondary aggregators (marked as such) where the official page was blocked, paywalled, or truncated during this session. Every number below is followed by a source link; where two sources disagree, both are shown.

Volumes used for the scenario math (given): **A = 900, B = 9,000, C = 90,000 voice minutes/month**, split 60% STT / 40% TTS, TTS at ~150 wpm ≈ 900 characters/minute of speech.

| Scenario | Total min | STT min (60%) | TTS min (40%) | TTS characters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 900 | 540 | 360 | 324,000 |
| B | 9,000 | 5,400 | 3,600 | 3,240,000 |
| C | 90,000 | 54,000 | 36,000 | 32,400,000 |

### 2a. Independent LatAm-Spanish medical benchmark (highest-value data point found)

A July 2026 medRxiv preprint, "Benchmarking Speech Recognition Models for Medical Consultations in Latin American Spanish," ran real medical-consultation audio through 10 STT systems, both commercial and open-source ([medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.14.26358062v1.full)):

| Model | WER | CER | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini-2.5-pro | 14.0% | 9.0% | Best closed-source performer in this study |
| gpt-4o-transcribe, gpt-4o-mini-transcribe, ElevenLabs Scribe v2, AssemblyAI | evaluated | evaluated | Exact WER/CER not extracted from the available excerpt — **gap, needs the full paper/supplementary tables** |
| Whisper Large v3 | 18.6% | 11.8% | Best open-source model; selected for fine-tuning |
| Whisper Large, Whisper Large v3 Turbo, Voxtral Mini 3B, Canary-1B-v2 | evaluated | not broken out | — |
| Fine-tuned Whisper Large v3 | — | — | Outperformed baselines on BLEU (63%), BERTScore (89%), semantic similarity (89%) on held-out video |

**Deepgram, Google Cloud STT, Azure Speech, and AWS Transcribe were not included in this study** — a real gap in independently verified Spanish-medical accuracy for the three major clouds and for Deepgram specifically.

## 4. Scenario cost modeling — assumptions

- All three stacks below price the **streaming/real-time SKU** on the STT side (a voice companion is inherently interactive), and the standard TTS voice tier appropriate to each vendor.
- Deepgram figures use the **current promotional PAYG price**; the regular (post-promotion) price is shown as a sensitivity column since Deepgram's own pricing page explicitly labels the lower number as time-limited ([Deepgram Pricing](https://deepgram.com/pricing)).
- Google's stack prices **Cloud Speech-to-Text v2 (Chirp)** as the headline STT choice because its HIPAA/BAA path is better established than Gemini 3.5 Transcribe's (see gap noted in §2); the cheaper Gemini 3.5 Transcribe price is shown as an alternative once that BAA question is resolved.
- Free character tiers are applied before per-character billing, since scenario A in particular falls entirely inside several vendors' free TTS allowances.
- None of the three stacks below is self-hosted: at these volumes (15–1,500 audio-hours/month of interactive, short-turn traffic) the batch-optimized GPU cost-per-hour numbers in §2/§3 (e.g., SaladCloud's $0.005/audio-hour for Whisper) are not achievable, because they assume continuous, saturating batch throughput, not sporadic real-time turns with cold-start and idle-GPU overhead. No GPU host currently exists for this product (per project context), so self-hosting would add infrastructure lead time and ops burden on top of compute cost.

## 6. Confidence and gaps

**High confidence (official page fetched directly, numbers internally consistent):**
- Deepgram STT/TTS pricing, keyterm prompting add-on, free credit ([deepgram.com/pricing](https://deepgram.com/pricing)).
- Deepgram Nova-3 Medical is English-only ([developers.deepgram.com](https://developers.deepgram.com/docs/models-languages-overview)) — directly contradicts an assumption that a medical STT model would cover Spanish; **no researched vendor offers an English+Spanish medical STT model except Speechmatics and AssemblyAI Medical Mode.**
- AssemblyAI pricing, Medical Mode surcharge and languages, self-serve BAA language ([assemblyai.com/pricing](https://www.assemblyai.com/pricing)).
- OpenAI STT/TTS pricing (official, via redirect) ([developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing)).
- AWS Transcribe Medical is English (US) only and costs $0.075/min ([aws.amazon.com/transcribe/pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/transcribe/pricing/); [Whipscribe](https://whipscribe.com/tools/aws-transcribe-medical)).
- Nova-3 Medical's WER/KER claims, and the LatAm-Spanish medical WER/CER numbers for Gemini-2.5-pro and Whisper Large v3, both come from named, dated sources.

**Medium confidence (consistent across 2+ independent secondary sources, but no official page was fetchable):**
- Speechmatics per-minute pricing (official page didn't expose a rate table; two aggregators roughly agree).
- Groq Whisper large-v3-turbo pricing and free-tier limits (official `groq.com/pricing` page had no pricing table when fetched; aggregator numbers only).
- Google Cloud TTS per-character pricing and free tiers (official page truncated twice; two aggregators agree closely).
- AWS Transcribe **standard** pricing is **internally inconsistent between sources**: the official page (as fetched) showed $0.006/min batch / $0.01/min streaming, while a secondary tiered-pricing aggregator showed $0.024/min declining with volume to $0.0078/min. These may reflect different pricing eras, different regions, or a scraping/aggregation error. **Do not commit to either number without a direct, complete re-fetch of `aws.amazon.com/transcribe/pricing` or an AWS Pricing Calculator run.**
- Azure Neural/Neural HD TTS pricing (per-character rate came from a Microsoft Community Hub post and a third-party tracker, not the raw Azure pricing calculator, which returned placeholder values in this session).

**Low confidence / not found — recommend direct vendor confirmation before committing:**
- Whether Azure AI Speech (STT and TTS) is explicitly in-scope under Microsoft's standard HIPAA BAA amendment — sources agree the BAA is "not automatic per service" but none named Speech explicitly in-scope or out-of-scope.
- Whether Google's **Gemini 3.5 Transcribe** (Gemini Developer API SKU) is covered by any BAA at all, as distinct from Vertex AI's Cloud Speech-to-Text — this materially changes whether Google Stack's cheaper alternative pricing is usable for this clinical product.
- Cartesia's HIPAA/BAA status — no search result addressed it directly.
- Exact current es-MX voice lists for Google Cloud TTS and Amazon Polly (both official pricing/voice pages were inaccessible or truncated in this session).
- Self-hosted Chatterbox GPU cost per hour, and any Spanish-specific (as opposed to aggregate/English-weighted) quality comparison for Kokoro, Piper, or Chatterbox.
- Full WER/CER table from the medRxiv LatAm-Spanish study for gpt-4o-transcribe, gpt-4o-mini-transcribe, ElevenLabs Scribe v2, AssemblyAI, Whisper Large, Whisper Large v3 Turbo, Voxtral Mini 3B, and Canary-1B-v2 — the fetched excerpt confirmed these models were tested but did not surface their individual scores.
- Real-time, interactive-turn latency figures (as opposed to marketing TTFA or batch-throughput RTF) for nearly every provider; Cartesia was the only vendor where a "marketed vs. measured" latency gap was explicitly reported (90–40ms marketed vs. ~166–190ms measured).

**Structural caveat on self-hosting economics:** every attractive self-hosted GPU-cost number found this session (Whisper at $0.005/audio-hour, Parakeet at $0.0013/audio-hour) comes from large-batch benchmarks with continuous GPU saturation. A bilingual voice companion serving 15–1,500 hours/month of short, real-time turns will not reach that utilization, and there is currently no GPU host in this project's infrastructure, so realistic self-hosted economics at these scenario sizes were not established and should not be assumed comparable to the hosted-API numbers above.
