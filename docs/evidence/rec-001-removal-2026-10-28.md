## Behavior and decisions

- Origin is explicit: Uploaded by you / Uploaded by your care team, with an
  unavailable fallback for unknown or inconsistent metadata.
- Direct removal is limited to the signed-in patient's own pending, unused upload.
  The server checks source review/annotations, active extraction/summary runs,
  candidate decisions/audit, care-plan references and canonical source links.
  A failed eligibility read cannot expose a Remove button.
- Migration 101 locks the owned document, then tries the established patient
  evidence fence without waiting in reverse lock order. Pending-candidate cleanup,
  metadata removal and minimal audit commit together. A denial makes no withdrawal
  or clinical mutation. Repeated and simultaneous removals use one audit entry for
  replay. Best-effort private storage cleanup happens after commit, and checks the
  patient's namespace again. The audit stores IDs/timestamp, not document text.
- A transient evidence conflict refreshes eligibility for a new explicit attempt;
  it does not retry DELETE automatically or pretend a clinician reviewed the file.
- Reviewed/used/rejected/clinician sources offer a private care-team request. The
  patient must choose a recipient, prepare an owned-source message and select Send
  through the existing conversation flow. An unrelated or uncertain draft is never
  overwritten. Request text identifies the source; no AI explanation is attached.
  This is a normal private message with existing delivery/audit behavior, not a new
  queue or a claim that a correction/removal has already happened.
- The explanation uses a neutral surface, quiet AI chip, a short opening and spaced
  detail bullets. Repeated visible category headings and duplicated introductory
  copy are removed. Supplied questions remain separate from factual details, with
  accessible list names. One scope/safety notice remains. No content is truncated.
